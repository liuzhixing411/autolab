## autolab: when to use this and where the output goes
- Use it when a direction fails its GO rule, before proposing a pivot, at a retrospective, or when the human asks for new directions. The lenses "failure analysis and boundary probing", "simplicity test", "composition and decomposition" (find the real bottleneck) and "what changed?" usually matter most after a negative result.
- Ground every candidate in the project's own evidence: profiles, rejected claims, `state/DECISIONS.md`, `state/HYPOTHESES.md`. Write all candidates, including the rejected ones with their kill reason, to `state/ideation/<date>-<topic>.md`.
- Each surviving idea gets: the H-id it would test (add it to `state/HYPOTHESES.md`), the cheapest offline measurement or pilot that could kill it, and a pre-registered GO rule.
- Choosing a direction is a PI decision (`autolab decide`); a stop or major pivot needs the human first (RULES).
